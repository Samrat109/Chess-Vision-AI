# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of **ChessVision AI**, are committed to making participation in our project a respectful, welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all experience levels, backgrounds, and skill sets.

Our goal is to create a positive community where developers, chess enthusiasts, testers, and open-source contributors can collaborate and learn together.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and considerate toward others.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Accepting different opinions and technical approaches.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community.
- Giving credit to other contributors for their work.
- Communicating professionally and collaboratively.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or humiliating other contributors.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate intimidation, or unwanted attention.
- Sharing offensive or inappropriate content.
- Disrupting discussions or community collaboration.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and issues.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Project discussions.
- Community communication channels.
- Any situation where someone represents ChessVision AI or its community.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to maintain a safe and respectful environment.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it to the project maintainers.

You can report issues through the project's GitHub repository.

When reporting an incident, include:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant links or screenshots, if available.
- The date and context of the incident.
- Any additional information that may help the maintainers investigate.

Reports will be reviewed respectfully and handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers will review reports and determine appropriate action based on the circumstances.

Possible actions include:

1. A warning and request to correct the behavior.
2. Temporary restrictions on participation.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Permanent removal from project participation in serious or repeated cases.

Maintainers are expected to act fairly, consistently, and professionally.
